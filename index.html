About the house

Coffee, shade, and the hum of the street

Seven7een sits behind a wall of green — fairy lights strung through the branches, low wooden tables, and a menu built for lingering. It's the kind of place you come for one coffee and stay for three.

By evening the terrace turns into Cairo's living room: shisha coals glowing, football on the screens, and a kitchen that keeps going well past midnight.

  • Open-air garden terrace
  • Shisha & premium tobacco
  • Live sports on screens
  • Free high-speed WiFi
  • Family & group friendly
  • Kitchen open till late
